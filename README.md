# Dean H. Stanton

**Engineering & Operations Executive | AI/LLM Evaluation | Enterprise Systems | PMP | Author**

I work at the intersection of business operations, engineering, technology, and AI. My background includes leading manufacturing operations, engineering and quality teams, managing enterprise software initiatives, and translating business requirements into systems that people can actually use.

I served as a GM/COO in medical device manufacturing and now work in project management and consulting. I also teach project management as an adjunct professor at Southern New Hampshire University. I'm particularly interested in evaluating AI systems against real operational requirements, where a plausible answer isn't necessarily a correct or usable one.

## Areas of expertise

- **Manufacturing and operations:** production, quality systems, supply chain, purchasing, inventory, and process improvement
- **Enterprise technology:** MES/MOM, ERP, WMS, QMS, CRM, systems integration, workflow design, and requirements analysis
- **Project and program management:** cross-functional leadership, planning, execution, stakeholder communication, and risk management
- **AI evaluation:** domain-expert review, requirements-based testing, analysis of model outputs, and practical AI adoption in business workflows
